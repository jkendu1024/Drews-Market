Frequently Asked Questions

Catering · Butcher · Deli

How are butcher prices calculated?

The final price of butcher items is determined by final weight once prepared. Prices are subject to change without notice.

Is a deposit required?

Yes — a non-refundable deposit is required for all butcher orders. The final balance, less deposits, is paid at pick-up.

How do I place a catering or holiday order?

Call us at (732) 974-9399 or email drewsmarketspringlake@gmail.com. Holiday orders fill up quickly, so order early.

Do you deliver?

Please call to discuss delivery for your event.
